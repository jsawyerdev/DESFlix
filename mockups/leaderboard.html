How the engagement score is computed
Weighted combination of completion rate, rewatch rate, and reputation-weighted rating — recomputed on a rolling 7-day decay window so the table reflects recent demand, not lifetime totals. Raw paid-rating count is deliberately not a direct input and is not shown, per docs/tokenomics.md §5 (avoids advertising the exact number an attacker would want to farm). Trend percentages are estimates over a short window and carry wider uncertainty at low sample sizes — shown explicitly above rather than presented with false precision.
What moves this ranking
Completion, rewatch, reputation-weighted rating. Not raw paid-rating count.
Decay window
7 days — reflects "what people want more of now," not an all-time list.
Placement eligibility
Same ranking signal gates advertiser bids — one metric, not two separately-gameable ones.
Mockup only. Scores, sample sizes, and trend percentages are illustrative placeholders, not a proposed scoring formula.
